About Clarity Retail

Clarity Retail is a modern hardware inventory management system built specifically for African retail environments. It works online and offline, supports optional fiscalisation, and delivers real-time multi‑branch visibility. From stock takes to sales analytics, everything is designed to be minimalistic yet powerful - with clear reports and support for mobile money, cash, and cards.

Offline-first architecture ensures business continuity even without internet.
